A few good questions.

Not sure where to begin? Here are the essentials before you send your request.

hello@velora.example
Do you carry out the cleaning?

No. We are an independent discovery platform. Cleaning services are provided by independent businesses, with whom you agree and book directly.

Can I request carpet and rug cleaning together?

Yes. Select both services in your message and tell us what needs attention. A provider can confirm whether they can handle both.

How much will it cost?

Pricing depends on size, material, condition, access and the work required. Your provider supplies the quote before you decide to book.

Can every stain be removed?

Not always. Fibre damage, age and previous treatments can affect results. Ask your provider to assess the stain and explain realistic expectations.

Do I have to book after requesting an introduction?

No. Submitting a request is not a booking or a payment commitment. You choose whether to proceed with a provider.
