A simple approach

This website uses browser storage to remember your cookie preference. No analytics or advertising cookies are enabled by default.

What is stored

The local-storage key velora-cookie-consent records whether you selected Accept or Essential only, together with the time of your choice. It is stored on your device, not submitted through the enquiry form.

Essential preferences

Both choices allow us to remember your selection and keep the banner from reappearing on every page. Accept does not activate hidden marketing or tracking services.

Change your choice

Use Cookie preferences in the footer to reopen the banner. You can also remove this preference through your browser’s site-data settings.
